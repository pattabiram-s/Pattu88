Observability

Instrument before you optimise

You cannot tune what you cannot see. Measure first, then let the data — not a hunch — point at the bottleneck.

Ship the signals that explain behaviour

Structured logs, the four golden signals (latency, traffic, errors, saturation), and a trace ID that follows a request across services. Together they turn ‘it feels slow’ into a specific span on a specific dependency.

Correlate, don’t just collect

A log, a metric, and a trace that can’t be joined are three dead ends. Thread a request ID through everything so you can pivot from a spiking metric to the exact traces and logs behind it.

Let data kill the hunch

Optimising before measuring is how teams spend a week tuning the thing that wasn’t the problem. Find the real bottleneck in the trace, change one thing, and watch the same signal to confirm it moved.

What ‘good’ looks like

From an alert, you can answer ‘what changed, where, and for whom’ in minutes — and you delete dashboards no one reads instead of adding more.
