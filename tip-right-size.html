Cost

Right-size on real usage, not guesses

The steady, unglamorous wins — autoscaling, switching off idle non-prod, tagging everything — beat any one big migration.

Size from what it actually uses

Provision from observed CPU, memory, and traffic, not the number that felt safe. Over-provisioning ‘to be safe’ is the single largest line item on most cloud bills, and it hides in plain sight.

Turn off what no one is using

Autoscale production to demand and schedule non-prod to shut down nights and weekends. Dev and staging that run 24/7 to serve a 9-to-6 team are paying triple for idle time.

Make the bill accountable

Tag every resource with an owner, environment, and service so the invoice tells you who owns what. Untagged spend is unowned spend, and unowned spend only grows.

Review on a rhythm

A monthly half-hour over idle resources, orphaned volumes, and forgotten load balancers recovers more, more reliably, than a once-a-year re-architecture. Small and regular beats big and rare.
