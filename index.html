About

I started out racking servers and answering support calls, and I never lost that operator's instinct. Over 14 years I grew into SRE and DevOps leadership, architecting, migrating, and running production platforms across AWS, Azure, DigitalOcean, and on-premise VMware.

The thread through all of it is reliability. I build CI/CD pipelines, containerise stubborn legacy applications, stand up Kubernetes clusters, and wire up monitoring so problems surface before users feel them. I care just as much about cost, disaster recovery, and security as I do about uptime.

How I work

  • Design for the bad day. Load balancing, failover, and disaster recovery are in from the start, not bolted on later.
  • Automate the repeatable. Infrastructure as code and pipelines beat manual, error-prone steps every time.
  • Measure before optimising. Every system gets metrics, logs, and alerts I actually trust.
  • Respect the bill. Right-sized, waste-free infrastructure is part of good engineering.
