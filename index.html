About

I'm a critical-thinking AWS architect with a deep understanding of high-availability architecture. Over 14 years I've grown from systems administration into SRE and DevOps leadership — architecting, migrating, and operating production platforms across AWS, Azure, DigitalOcean, and on-prem VMware.

My work centers on continuous delivery, containerization, and reliability: building CI/CD pipelines, containerizing legacy apps, standing up Kubernetes clusters, and wiring up monitoring so teams can ship confidently and recover fast. I'm equally comfortable driving cost optimization, disaster recovery, and security hardening.

  • Design for high availability. Load balancing, failover, and DR built in from the start.
  • Automate everything. IaC and pipelines over manual, error-prone steps.
  • Observe before you optimize. Metrics, logs, and alerts on every system.
  • Optimize for cost. Right-sized, Fargate/spot-aware, waste-free infrastructure.
