About

I sit at the intersection of business intent and technical reality. My job is to hear a goal — often fuzzy, always constrained — and shape it into an architecture with clear trade-offs, honest risks, and a path to production.

I treat scalability, reliability, security, cost, and maintainability as first-class citizens, not afterthoughts. I document decisions (ADRs), draw the system before I build it, and prefer boring, proven technology unless novelty genuinely earns its keep.

  • Clarity before code. Requirements and constraints first, diagrams second, implementation third.
  • Design for failure. Every single point of failure named and mitigated.
  • Cost is a design axis. The most elegant system nobody can afford is a failed system.
  • Leave it maintainable. The team that inherits it should thank you, not curse you.
