About

I'm a critical-thinking AWS architect with a strong grounding in high-availability architecture. Over 14 years I've grown from systems administration into SRE and DevOps leadership, architecting, migrating, and operating production platforms across AWS, Azure, DigitalOcean, and on-premise VMware.

My work centres on continuous delivery, containerisation, and reliability. I build CI/CD pipelines, containerise legacy applications, stand up Kubernetes clusters, and wire up monitoring so teams can ship confidently and recover quickly. I also lead cost optimisation, disaster recovery, and security hardening.

  • Design for high availability. Load balancing, failover, and disaster recovery built in from the start.
  • Automate the repeatable. Infrastructure as code and pipelines over manual, error-prone steps.
  • Measure before optimising. Metrics, logs, and alerts on every system.
  • Keep costs in view. Right-sized, waste-free infrastructure.
