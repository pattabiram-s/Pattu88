DevSecOps

Shift security left, but gate at the right

Early feedback and a hard production gate are complements, not a choice — you want both.

Make issues cheap to fix

Scan dependencies, containers, and IaC inside the pull request, where a fix is a quick edit and a re-push. The earlier a problem surfaces, the less it costs and the more likely it is to actually get fixed.

Still enforce at the boundary

Shifting left reduces how much reaches production; it doesn’t replace admission control and runtime enforcement. Block unsigned images, deny risky configs at admission, and keep watching at runtime — defence in depth, not a single checkpoint.

Gates must be fast and fair

A security gate that is slow or noisy gets bypassed. Keep scans quick, tune out the false positives, and make the required fix obvious. A trusted gate is one people route through, not around.

What ‘good’ looks like

Developers see security findings as they code, production refuses what policy forbids, and neither depends on a human remembering to check.
