Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This is the personal portfolio website of Pattabi Ram S ("I", "me"). This page explains how the site handles information. The site is intentionally simple and does not try to identify or track its visitors.

Information I collect

This website does not collect personal information. There are no sign-up forms, no accounts, and no contact forms that store data. If you choose to contact me using the email or phone links provided, you share that information with me directly, and I use it only to reply to you.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set its own cookies and does not run analytics or advertising trackers.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process limited technical information such as your IP address and browser type in its server logs for security and operational purposes. This is handled by GitHub under its own privacy practices, which you can review on the GitHub site policy pages.

Third-party links and fonts

Pages may link to external sites such as LinkedIn and GitHub. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and I am not responsible for their content or practices. The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts; your browser requests those files from Google when a page loads.

Your choices

Because the site does not collect personal data, there is nothing for you to update or delete here. If you have emailed me and would like me to delete that correspondence, let me know and I will do so.

Contact
